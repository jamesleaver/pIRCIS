# Support

pIRCIS is one person's project, so support is a GitHub issue:

https://github.com/jamesleaver/pIRCIS/issues

Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot
helps, and so does the program, which the app can share as plain text
(**PROG > Share this program**).

There is no account and nothing to sign in to, so nothing can be reset on
your behalf; everything the app has is on your device, in its own folder in
the Files app.

## Before you ask

- **A program does nothing:** the cell the runner starts on may be blank. A
  runner sets off over empty cells and leaves the grid without ever reaching
  a command. Start it with an arrow.
- **A program stops with a message:** the readout under the grid
  (**SYS > UNDER GRID: RUNNERS**) says what each runner is about to do; step
  through with **STEP BUTTONS** on and the line before the stop is the one to
  look at.
- **Learning the language:** the guide is at
  https://github.com/jamesleaver/pIRCIS/blob/main/LEARN.md, one tap away
  from **SYS > LEARN IRCIS**.
