# Privacy

pIRCIS collects no data.

It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not connect to
the internet, except to open a web page in your browser when you tap a link
inside the app. Programs you save or import stay in the app's own folder on
your device, which you can see and manage in the Files app. Nothing leaves
the device unless you share a program yourself.

If you have a question, open an issue at
https://github.com/jamesleaver/pIRCIS.
