Privacy

pIRCIS collects no data.

It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It does not connect to the internet, except to open a web page in your browser when you tap a link inside the app. Programs you save or import stay in the app's own folder on your device, which you can see and manage in the Files app. Nothing leaves the device unless you share a program yourself.

The version that runs in a web browser, on the pIRCIS website, is the same. Programs and settings you save there are kept in that browser's own storage on your device, and are not sent anywhere.

If you have a question, open an issue at https://github.com/jamesleaver/pIRCIS.
